Taking Care of Your Aquarium

Some basic ways to keep your tank clean and your fish healthy.

Regular Maintenance

Once your aquarium is set up, you still have to take care of it. Fish produce waste and leftover food can make the water dirty over time.

Regular water changes help keep the tank cleaner and make it a better environment for your fish. It is also important to check the filter and make sure everything is working correctly.

Basic Cleaning Steps

  1. Remove some of the old aquarium water.
  2. Clean debris out of the gravel.
  3. Check the filter.
  4. Add treated water back into the tank.
  5. Make sure all equipment is working.
