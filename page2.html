Aquarium Care

Basic tips for keeping your aquarium clean and your fish healthy.

Home Aquarium Care

Water Changes

Regular water changes are important because waste can build up in an aquarium over time. Replacing part of the water helps keep the tank cleaner.

It is usually better to change part of the water instead of completely replacing all of it.

Feeding Fish

Fish should be given the correct type and amount of food. Too much food can create extra waste and make the water dirty.

Researching the fish species you own can help you decide what food is best for them.

Basic Care Tips

  • Use a filter.
  • Check the water regularly.
  • Do regular water changes.
  • Avoid overfeeding.
  • Research fish before adding them.
