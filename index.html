Getting Started

Fishkeeping can be a fun hobby, but it is important to set up the aquarium correctly before adding fish. Choosing the right tank, filter, and decorations can help create a healthy environment.

One of the biggest mistakes beginners make is adding fish too quickly. A properly prepared aquarium can help keep fish healthy and reduce problems.

Choosing Fish

Different fish need different tank sizes, temperatures, and water conditions. Before buying a fish, it is important to research what that species needs.

Some popular beginner fish include bettas, tetras, guppies, and rasboras.
